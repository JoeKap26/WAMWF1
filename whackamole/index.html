How to Play

Listen to the word, then tap the mole with the matching word! You can also tap the floating letter that matches the first letter of the word!

Earn Points

  • Correct Word: +10 Bonus Points and Level Up!
  • Correct Floating Letter: +5 Bonus Points.
  • 5 Correct Words in a Row: +50 Bonus Points!
  • 10 Correct Words in a Row: +200 Bonus Points!

Special Powers

  • ❄️ Freeze: Earn Freeze Power after 3 correct words in a row. Tap to freeze everything for 5 seconds!
  • Magic W: Catch the floating W to reveal the correct word.
  • Magic L: Catch the floating L to reveal the correct letter.

Wrong Answers

  • Wrong Letter: Wait 2 seconds before trying again.
  • Wrong Word: Wait 3 seconds before trying again.

Listen, find the word, whack the mole, and have fun!
